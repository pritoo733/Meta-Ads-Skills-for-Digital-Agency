# Validate the offer and decide from results

Use this worksheet for a new campaign, a stalled funnel, or a request for a chance of success. Complete a decision record from available evidence; leave unknowns explicit. Readiness, observed conversion rates, and future business success are different things.

## Validate demand before final creative

Record the buyer, costly problem, urgency, current alternative, service deliverables, terms, and why the agency can deliver. Match important assumptions to customer interviews, sales-call notes, lost-deal reasons, prior purchases, or comparable first-party campaign results. Record dates and contradictory evidence. Competitor ads and video case studies suggest hypotheses; their existence does not validate this offer.

Review buyer conversations for what triggered the search, what they tried, why it failed in their words, who approves spending, and what would make the service worth paying for. Distinguish compliments from booking, attendance, proposal acceptance, and payment. Choose the amount of research from the uncertainty being resolved; do not impose a universal interview count.

If demand or proof is weak, choose the smallest useful test: validate package and price in buyer conversations, clarify a misunderstood deliverable, or run a capped Meta pilot. Describe an unproven offer honestly. Ads begin after offer approval and the critical operating gates pass.

## Complete the success contract

| Field | Record before launch |
| --- | --- |
| Primary outcome | Qualified booked appointments for acquisition feedback; qualified attended calls and profitable clients for business evaluation. Specify qualification criteria. |
| Evaluation cohort | Leads acquired in a named date range, followed until the booking and sales-cycle cutoff. Include currency, timezone, source, and deduplication rule. |
| Commercial target | Maximum fully loaded CAC, media CAC allowance, contribution horizon, payback deadline, and minimum quality criteria. |
| Capacity | Available slots, response owner and service window, sales capacity, and onboarding capacity. |
| Pilot limit | Authorized total spend cap, maximum affordable loss, review date or evidence condition, and decision owner. |
| Hypothesis | One bottleneck, proposed change, expected mechanism, and outcome that would support or reject it. |
| Decision rule | Conditions for continue, revise, stop, and scale, defined before results arrive. |

If maximum fully loaded CAC is `A` and non-media acquisition cost per client is `N`, media CAC allowance is `M = A - N`. If costs cannot be allocated per client, model total acquisition costs instead; do not subtract them twice. A zero or negative allowance means current economics do not support paid acquisition.

With close rate `c = clients / qualified attended calls` and show rate `s = qualified attended / qualified booked`, target media cost per attended call is `M × c`; target media cost per qualified booking is `M × c × s`. Use comparable mature cohorts. Without usable history, show conservative, central, and optimistic assumptions and resulting targets; these are scenarios. Check cash timing and contribution after acquisition costs as well as CAC.

Arithmetic example only: a $600 media CAC allowance, 20% close rate, and 75% show rate imply $120 per qualified attended call and $90 per qualified booking. These are not recommended benchmarks. Pilot budget divided by target booking cost shows bookings affordable **if the target is achieved**, not a promised count. If the budget cannot yield decision-useful evidence at plausible costs, simplify the test, validate demand first, or revise the plan.

## Keep cohort data usable

Capture stable lead ID, acquisition date, campaign and creative identifiers when available, consent record, qualification decision and reason, confirmed booking ID and time, attendance, sales outcome and reason, collected revenue, and outcome timestamps. Keep pending and unknown outcomes explicit. Log response time and contact attempts to distinguish handling from ad quality. Minimize personal data; keep sensitive details out of advertising event payloads.

Report an acquisition cohort through its outcomes. Do not divide this week's sales by this week's appointments when the sales came from older leads. For show rate, include bookings whose scheduled occurrence has passed under the stated rescheduling rule; future appointments are pending. For close rate, show open opportunities separately and apply the agreed decision horizon. Count a rescheduled appointment once consistently. Reconcile missing source fields and duplicates before attributing creative performance.

Show counts beside rates; use `not estimable` for zero denominators. Two clients from ten attended calls is not a 20% chance another agency will succeed. If uncertainty estimates are useful, calculate a justified interval for the specific stage rate and state its sample assumptions; do not rename it an agency-success probability. Never present readiness as a percentage chance of winning.

## Choose the next action

| Decision | Evidence and action |
| --- | --- |
| Continue within the cap | The path works, quality is acceptable, evidence is limited or the cohort is maturing, and remaining authorized spend can answer the hypothesis. State the next review condition. |
| Revise and retest | Evidence identifies a constraint. Inspect disqualification reasons, booking failures, no-shows, or sales objections; fix that constraint and set a bounded test. |
| Stop or pause | Cap reached, economics outside agreed tolerance, critical gate failed, or capacity unavailable. Distinguish a rejected hypothesis from insufficient evidence. Do not extend spend silently. |
| Scale in bounded steps | Mature cohorts meet quality, CAC, contribution, and payback targets; tracking reconciles and capacity is available. Recheck marginal cost and quality after each increase. |

Do not declare success or failure from a few early outcomes or a platform metric alone. Insufficient evidence does not authorize unlimited spend. If a test ends without bookings, inspect delivery and the earliest failed handoff. Do not relax qualification to make numbers look better; investigate whether criteria reflect real service fit and revise only with client approval.

Use image and video ads around the approved offer with distinct buyer-relevant angles and evidence. Unequal platform delivery does not establish a causal format winner. Use a supported controlled experiment when that question matters; otherwise label the comparison observational. Test the verified bottleneck instead of changing offer, audience, page, and reminders together.

Return: **decision → cohort counts and economics → strongest evidence → uncertainty → one next action → owner → spend cap → review condition**. Distinguish configured settings from verified live behavior and measured results.
